[ STUDIO ORIGINS & VISION ]

A Dedicated Atelier for Quiet, Monumental Spaces

Founded in 2011, Zero Design operates at the intersection of monolithic architectural minimalism, ancient craft traditions, and carbon-negative futures.

[ THE ZERO MANIFESTO ]

Why "Zero"?

In mathematics and Zen philosophy, Zero is not emptiness—it is the fertile origin from which all infinite form arises.

We established Zero Design as an antidote to decorative noise and hyper-commoditized architectural sprawl. We believe spaces should calm the nervous system, reflect the passage of sunlight, and honor the unvarnished truth of raw materials.

Every dwelling, pavilion, or spatial brand environment we create begins with complete listening to the site's microclimate, geological history, and cultural roots.
